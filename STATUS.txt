TECHNICAL NOTES

Architecture:
  Webcam → OpenCV → [Pose + Face + YOLO] → Engagement Engine → Dashboard

Performance:
  Target: 15+ FPS on Apple M5

Deployment:
  - Edge computing (no cloud required)
  - Zero bandwidth after model download
  - Privacy-friendly (no data collection)
  - Scales to 700k cameras
